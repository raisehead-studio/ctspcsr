Active Implementation of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

In 2015, the United Nations adopted a total of 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and 169 specific targets, which are provided to countries as a reference guide for policy transformation. The CTSP Bureau follows the global trend, integrates with the world, and uses its core capabilities to respond to the SDGs and the 16 corresponding Sustainable Development Goals of Taiwan as well as specific practices, allowing interested parties to understand the sustainable business results of the CTSP Bureau.

Putting the SDGs into practice, part 1 (English text version of the Chinese chart)

SDGs, Taiwan SDGs indicators and CTSP actions (part 1)
SDGTaiwan SDGs indicatorActions
SDG 1 No poverty1.4.1 Entrepreneurship workshops held; 1.4.2 People receiving consulting and coachingAs of 2021, 19 startups had moved into the innovation and entrepreneurship space; 235 FITI teams had been coached in total, 97 of which have formed companies.
SDG 3 Good health and well-being3.9.1 Improve air quality and protect public healthA neighbourhood health-care programme in Houli Park offers free check-ups to eligible residents: 316 outreach sessions, 6,660 phone interviews and 4,918 health checks in 2011–2021. Epidemiological follow-up surveys (every 5 years) have covered 2,235 questionnaires and blood tests.
SDG 4 Quality education4.3.1 Participation of youth and adults in higher education in the past 12 monthsThe CTSP professional and technical talent training programme ran 36 courses in 5 categories with 811 trainees. The 2021 academic-year talent subsidy programme funded 12 course modules and internships at 9 schools, NT$8 million in total, for an expected 890 trainees.
SDG 4 Quality education4.7.4 Certified environmental education facilities3 wastewater treatment plants under CTSP are certified environmental education facilities, as are facilities of park companies TSMC and AUO.
SDG 5 Gender equality5.4.1 Gap in unpaid domestic and care work between married or cohabiting women and their partnersThe Bureau set up an employment discrimination and gender equality in employment committee and held 3 awareness sessions in 2021.
SDG 6 Clean water and sanitation6.4.3 Process water recycling rate of park companies; 6.4.5 Level of water stressIn 2021 the process water recycling rate (semiconductor and optoelectronics) reached over 85% in every park. The Bureau continues to provide guidance and promotion on water saving and recycling.
SDG 7 Affordable and clean energy7.2.1 Installed renewable energy capacityCTSP and park companies promote solar power; installed solar capacity in the parks exceeded 52.88 MW in 2021.

Putting the SDGs into practice, part 2 (English text version of the Chinese chart)

SDGs, Taiwan SDGs indicators and CTSP actions (part 2)
SDGTaiwan SDGs indicatorActions
SDG 8 Decent work and economic growth8.1.1 Economic growth rateContinued investment by park companies brought 2021 revenue to NT$1,035.232 billion, up 10.60% from 2020.
SDG 8 Decent work and economic growth8.6.1 Youth in vocational training and post-training employment rate; 8.6.2 Youth job referrals by public employment services43 recruitment events were held in 2021; employment reached 52,888 in December, up 2.05% from 51,827 a year earlier.
SDG 9 Industry, innovation and infrastructure9.1.1 Growth in road public transport ridershipFree shuttle buses in CTSP carried 87,944 passengers in 2021.
SDG 10 Reduced inequalities10.3.1 Public awareness of gender equality and anti-discrimination lawsIn 2021 the employment discrimination and gender equality committee met once and 3 labour law awareness sessions were held.
SDG 11 Sustainable cities and communities11.11.1 Gbps broadband coverage of 90% of householdsUnder the MOST smart park ICT programme, 179 Wi-Fi hotspots were completed, used about 1.32 million times (December 2016 to end of 2021).
SDG 12 Responsible consumption and production12.1.1 Companies passing green factory cleaner-production assessmentBy end of 2021, CTSP held 36 green building labels: 1 diamond-level smart building, 1 gold-level green building, 1 diamond-level eco-community, and 4 MOEA green factory labels.
SDG 12 Responsible consumption and production12.4.3 Reuse rate of park industrial wasteOver the past 7 years the reuse rate (including recycling) rose from 71.26% to 94.15%.
SDG 12 Responsible consumption and production12.6.3 Listed companies required to publish CSR reportsBy end of 2021, 52 CTSP companies published CSR reports.
SDG 12 Responsible consumption and production12.7.1 Green procurement ratio of government agenciesIn 2021 the Bureau spent NT$4.265 million on green products, 100% of total procurement.
SDG 13 Climate action13.1.1 Assess climate risks and implement priority adaptation plansIn 2021 the Bureau set up a climate change identification team to define response measures.
SDG 13 Climate action13.3.2 Promote behaviour change and local low-carbon action; 13.3.3 Build adaptive science capacity and servicesElectric shuttles in Huwei Park served 4,383 passengers over 10,700 km to 2021. Smart street lights cut about 1,350 t CO2e in 2018–2021.
